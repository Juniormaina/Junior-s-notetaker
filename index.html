Capture it. Keep it. Find it.

Notoza is a simple, modern note-taking app designed to help people capture, organize, and find their thoughts quickly. Notoza gives your thoughts a place to land.

Why Notoza?

We all have scattered thoughts across countless apps, notebooks, and digital sticky notes. Notoza exists to make note-taking quick, simple, and accessible, turning the chaos into clarity.

Our Approach

Simple by design

We believe note-taking should be frictionless. Notoza is being built with a core focus on speed and simplicity, removing the clutter so you can focus on writing.

Your thoughts stay yours

We're building Notoza with a local-first approach, so your notes can remain accessible even when you're offline.

Built for everyday life

Designed for the web first, with Android support planned for the future. Take your ideas with you wherever you go.

Our Mission

We are on a mission to make it easier for everyone to capture, organize, and revisit their ideas. Whether it's a fleeting thought or a grand project plan, we want to provide the canvas for your mind.
